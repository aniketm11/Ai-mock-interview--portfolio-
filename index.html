PROJECT WORKFLOW

One clear flow from candidate setup to report.

01

Candidate setup

Name, email, optional contact details, resume, target role, skills and job description.

02

AI questions

OpenAI creates role- and level-specific interview questions.

03

Live answer

Camera and microphone are used during the interview. Video is not saved.

04

Speech metrics

Transcript, word count, pace, fillers, pauses and hesitation signals are calculated.

05

Vision signals

Face presence, multiple faces, approximate gaze attention and looking-away events are measured.

06

AI report

Technical and communication feedback is combined with interview metrics and exported as a report.

LIVE ANALYSIS

Analyze observable signals, not hidden emotions.

Browser vision is used for practical interview signals such as face presence and approximate gaze attention. These are estimates and are not treated as measurements of honesty, emotion, intelligence or hiring suitability.

TECH STACK

Simple separation between browser, API and data.

The browser handles camera, microphone and interview UI. The backend keeps OpenAI credentials, authentication and database access off the client.

02

AI

03

Backend

Node.js + Express API for authentication, interviews and reports.

04

Database

MariaDB/MySQL for users, sessions, answers and integrity events.
