CORE FEATURES

Everything is built around the interview session.

✦
AI QUESTIONS

Questions based on the candidate's role.

Choose Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced or Expert. Add skills and a job description for more relevant questions.

◉
VOICE

Answer naturally.

Speech-to-text measures transcript length, pace, filler words, pauses and hesitation signals.

◎
VISION

Observe the session.

Face presence, multiple faces, approximate gaze attention and looking-away events are measured while the camera is active.

HOW IT WORKS

Setup. Interview. Report.

01

Enter candidate details

Name and email are required. Phone, LinkedIn, location and resume are optional.

02

Configure the interview

Select role, level, skills and job description. The AI builds the interview around the setup.

03

Practice and review

Answer using your voice, review live signals, then receive AI evaluation and a downloadable report.

LIVE ANALYSIS

Video is analyzed during the session. It is not saved.

Observable signals include face presence, multiple faces, approximate gaze attention, looking-away events, speech pace, word count, fillers and hesitations.
